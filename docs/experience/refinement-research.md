## 2. How Research Shaped the Overhaul

1. **Continuous Single-Canvas Brain Lifecycle (`LoadingScreen.tsx`, `BrainFlight.tsx`, `brain.ts`)**:
   - One WebGL2 `<BrainFlight />` canvas (`data-brain-model="budai-neural-43f44c9"`) owns the opening (`intro`), landing hero (`hero`), and neural scroll passage (`journey`).
   - Added depth-shaded cortical luminescence (`vDepth`), dual-harmonic radial thought waves, and bi-directional synaptic signal propagation (`FS_POINTS`, `FS_LINES`).
2. **Seamless Brain-to-Playground Transformation (`BrainFlight.tsx`, `PlaygroundArrival.tsx`, `playground-signal.ts`)**:
   - As the camera travels into the cortex (`#signal`), organic cortical folds smoothly give way to an ordered 3D digital intelligence lattice and converging axial pathways (`aRegion = -2`) that resolve into an inverse-projected 3D signal (`writePlaygroundSignal`) landing directly on the real `[data-playground-surface]` toolbar.
3. **Cohesive 3D Central Intelligence Core & Capability Pathways (`capability-engine.ts`, `information-field.ts`, `CapabilityMap.tsx`, `Capabilities.tsx`)**:
   - Replaced disconnected visuals with a faceted icosahedral **Central Intelligence Core**, counter-rotating gimballed signal rings, axial spine, **six curved `CatmullRomCurve3` capability pathways**, station rings, and travelling signal pulses into the six capability constellations (`Write`, `Automate`, `Decide`, `SV/EN`, `Memory`, `Vision & Voice`), plus a 3-tier **Access Architecture** with shared capability constellations and concentric orbital capacity rings.
4. **Connected Chapter Transitions (`developer-room.ts`, `world.ts`, `Waitlist.tsx`, `Footer.tsx`)**:
   - Structured signal corridor from Playground to Capabilities; clean Developer Room workspace (no instanced keyboard keys or under-desk tube cables) with a 6-pathway `BUDAI / INTELLIGENCE CORE` diagram on the left monitor; 3D **Next Signal Relay Core** + horizon wave rings in Waitlist; four spatial Roadmap milestones converging into the `#continuation` outro anchor.
