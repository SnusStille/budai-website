## Original brain, not a new substitute

`43f44c9` contains `components/effects/BrainFlight.tsx` and the removed `components/sections/BrainScrollStory.tsx`. The latter used a separate renderer, a private native-scroll listener and React progress state. Its position and true camera flight are restored, but not that duplication. The current pre-Playground slot contained HeroAsk and WhatIsBudAI; it did not contain the deployed preview's older “01 · Signal” copy. Those generic presentations are removed from that slot. Meaningful SV/EN, context and account-memory information stays in the real Playground, capabilities and plans.

The original procedural two-hemisphere/fold/brainstem/cerebellum surface and internal points are retained. A single root owner now renders one canvas across the timed opening, landing aperture and separate native-scroll neural passage. New internal synapses and axial light streams are original procedural geometry. Absolute camera poses enter and pass through the anatomy, then resolve into the existing product's environment. The story component owns semantic content and progress, never another loader, canvas or scroll loop.

The one loading system remains in root Providers. No route loading file, section or nested layout starts it. The loader gets its own small camera approach, five explicitly paced title prefixes and the existing bounded asset/failsafe handling. The story has no title-typing interface or busy/inert gate.
