## 2. The Five-Chapter "Signal Journey" Creative Direction

The entire BudAI experience now tells **one continuous visual and strategic story**:
> **"A thought becomes a signal. The signal gains context. Context becomes intelligence. Intelligence becomes action."**

1. **Chapter 01 — The Spark (`Hero.tsx` + `LoadingScreen.tsx`)**:
   - A luminous signal awakens inside the 3D computational intelligence core (`BUDAI / 01 · THE SPARK — "Every action starts with a signal."`).
   - Interactive feature chips (`Personal · write & plan`, `Business · automate`, `SV / EN`, `Memory & voice`) highlight 3D regions on hover/focus and dispatch live starter prompts to `#playground` on click.
2. **Chapter 02 — The Context (`BrainScrollStory.tsx` + `brain.ts`)**:
   - The signal enters a network of context (`02 · THE CONTEXT — "A signal gains context."`).
   - Explicitly communicates **Swedish + English** as two natural streams and **user-controlled memory** (saving only the facts the user chooses to keep, never everything automatically).
3. **Chapter 03 — Intelligence in Motion (`CapabilityMap.tsx` + `CapabilityStudioScreen.tsx` + `capability-engine.ts`)**:
   - Inside the shared 3D Signal Studio, each of the 6 capabilities (`Write`, `Automate`, `Decide`, `Swedish + English`, `Memory`, `Vision and voice`) triggers a distinct **3D geometric transformation** on the foreground studio deck alongside the real `CSS3DObject` Studio Screen:
     - `01 Write`: Layered editorial strata align in 3D space.
     - `02 Automate`: 4-gate sequential wave pulse across the studio nodes.
     - `03 Decide`: Multiple 3D nodes converge toward the central decision vector.
     - `04 Swedish + English`: Intertwined `SV-SE` (cyan) and `EN-US` (lavender) 3D helical ribbons exchange signals in parity.
     - `05 Memory`: User-selected durable layers stay elevated and illuminated while transient fragments remain low.
     - `06 Vision & Voice`: Harmonic visual/acoustic spectrum around the recurring 3D Signal Core.
   - Includes an interactive **Personal (`Basic & Consumer`) vs. Team (`Business`) Pathway Switch** (`[data-pathway-toggle]`) and live **SV / EN** reactivity.
4. **Chapter 04 — The Birth of Something Real (`Capabilities.tsx` `[data-access-architecture]` → `About.tsx` Builder)**:
   - As the user scrolls from the 6 capabilities into `Choose your way in`, the left and right 3D studio wing monitors unfold from behind the central monolith into a **3-screen panoramic pipeline** (`01 Basic Intake` → `02 Consumer Memory` → `03 Business Team Workspace`), and then hand off along the 3D trajectory into the **Builder (`#about`)** studio room where TypeScript source code (`ProgressiveEditor.tsx`) assembles live interfaces and workflows, with an interactive `"Try this prompt in Playground"` link.
5. **Chapter 05 — Your Idea Starts Here (`AIPlayground.tsx` + `Waitlist.tsx` + `Journey.tsx`)**:
   - The camera resolves directly into the live **BudAI Playground (`#playground`)** (`"You've seen what's possible — now make something of your own."`), backed by transparent guest (`10/day`) vs. free account (`35/day` + user-controlled memory, history, and vision) limits and the `BUDAI-EARLY-10` founding-member waitlist.

## 3. Ranked Product, Engineering & Copy Improvements (Top 20)

1. **[Implemented]** Deterministic viewport-ratio `camera.setViewOffset` in `lib/experience/world.ts` — eliminates vertical camera frustum reversal and post-Playground scroll jumps.
2. **[Implemented]** 1:1 native RAF scroll cadence (`scrolling = true`) in `lib/experience/scroll.ts` — eliminates 30ms frame skipping during active 60Hz/120Hz scrolling.
3. **[Implemented]** Real `CSS3DObject` `<CapabilityStudioScreen />` portaled via `WorldStage.tsx` onto the 3D Capabilities Studio Monolith — brings Builder's exact vector DOM quality to Capabilities.
4. **[Implemented]** Pre-tokenized progressive code slicing (`token.text.slice(0, take)`) in `CapabilityStudioScreen.tsx` and `CapabilityMap.tsx` — eliminates mid-word syntax color flipping.
5. **[Implemented]** Living 3D Signal Core (`OctahedronGeometry` + dual gimbals) + intertwined `SV / EN` 3D helical `TubeGeometry` ribbons + 6-mode geometric node transformations in `lib/experience/capability-engine.ts`.
6. **[Implemented]** Smooth passive pointer telemetry (`pointerX`, `pointerY`) in `lib/experience/scroll.ts` driving subtle 3D parallax in both the Capabilities Signal Studio and Builder Studio.
7. **[Implemented]** Interactive **Personal vs. Team Pathway Toggle (`[data-pathway-toggle]`)** in `CapabilityMap.tsx` & `CapabilityStudioScreen.tsx`.
8. **[Implemented]** Full bilingual `SV / EN` reactivity inside the 3D `CSS3DObject` `<CapabilityStudioScreen />`.
9. **[Implemented]** Interactive Hero feature chips (`Hero.tsx`) with keyboard accessibility (`role="button"`, `tabIndex={0}`) that dispatch curated prompts to `#playground`.
10. **[Implemented]** Interactive `"Try this prompt in Playground"` action in `About.tsx` connecting the Builder's illustrative example to the live Playground.
11. **[Implemented]** Replaced generic/exaggerated copy (`"become one of the best AIs ever"`, `"become your new ChatGPT"`) across `Capabilities.tsx`, `About.tsx`, and `Journey.tsx` with specific, credible Swedish-engineered positioning.
12. **[Implemented]** Transparent tier differentiation in `Capabilities.tsx` (`Basic`: free preview with `10/day` guest and `35/day` free account + memory/vision; `Consumer`: planned individual plan; `Business`: planned team plan with shared credits/workspaces).
13. **[Implemented]** Explicit communication of **user-controlled memory** (never automatic retention of everything) across `Hero.tsx`, `BrainScrollStory.tsx`, `Capabilities.tsx`, and `CapabilityStudioScreen.tsx`.
14. **[Implemented]** Honest labeling of illustrative 3D demonstrations (`ILLUSTRATIVE 3D DEMONSTRATION · NOT A LIVE AI REQUEST` and `Prepared example · not a live AI response`).
15. **[Implemented]** Bounded rate-limiter map pruning in `app/api/playground/route.ts` (`isRateLimited`) to prevent memory growth in long-running server instances.
16. **[Verified]** `ResizeObserver` on `document.body` in `lib/experience/scroll.ts` automatically recalculates section bounds when dynamic DOM elements expand/collapse.
17. **[Verified]** Pre-warming `WorldStage.tsx` as soon as the user enters `#playground` (`frame.chapter > 1.85`) so `#capabilities` is already initialized before scrolling into view.
18. **[Verified]** Zero-draw GPU sleep when `worldOpacityAt(frame) <= 0.001` or `closing >= 0.999` (`tests/experience.spec.ts` & `tests/outro.spec.ts`).
19. **[Verified]** Full reduced-motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`), Save-Data (`navigator.connection.saveData`), and WebGL context-loss fallbacks (`builder-fallback-workspace`, `capability-drawing-fallback`, `access-fallback`, `BrainPoster`).
20. **[Verified]** Complete SV / EN language toggle consistency across Navigation, Hero, Signal Tour, Playground, Capabilities, Plans, Builder, Waitlist, Roadmap, and Footer.
